**Cells walked:** 25 (21 owner, 4 helper) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Functioning as walked:** 10 yes / 14 partial / 1 no &nbsp;·&nbsp; **High-friction cells:** 6 (OW-2, OW-5, OW-6, OW-9, OW-10, OW-19) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Findings:** 26 — Type I: 3 · Type II: 3 (all in scope; F23 is the owner's known open item) · Type III: 20 (all in scope)

**Verdict: HIGH_FRICTION_NOT_SHIPPABLE** — as built at `51fc3fc`. The design is sound: no Type II finding rises to a usability regression, and every cell that fails does so for a reason that can be built away. But the owner cannot complete four of the recoveries that design §7.2 promises, through the page, and a keyboard-only or screen-reader owner cannot use the folder tree. A usability regression blocks ship exactly like a security one, and these are the "ugly failure modes" the review exists for. The verdict becomes SHIPPABLE when the six blockers (F1-F6) are built and their cells re-walked; F7-F13 should go in the same pass. The remaining findings are polish and can follow.

**Rationale.** What holds up: the happy path is short and honest (11 steps from a first visit including the Microsoft code, 8 once signed in, and the preview says what, how big, where and who can read it before anything is written). An interrupted run is recoverable in three clicks with nothing lost. Check this export names the file that changed, vanished or was planted, and a helper with no app can verify the folder with the README's own commands (`shasum -c` and the extra-files one-liner were run and behave as written). Refusal texts are mostly X2-shaped (what, why, remedy) and none leaked a token, a Graph URL or a trace. The deployment switch leaves no dead link. An export beside a running backup, a second window, a reload and a dashboard restart all end in a sentence, not a stack.

What fails is the road back, in four places. (1) A refusal raised by a card button (Finish as partial, Check and finish, Look again, Locate, This folder is gone, the three "Export ... again" buttons) is appended to the card and wiped by the history reload the same handler starts, so the owner sees nothing happen *(script)* (F1). (2) "Sign in again" names a control that is not there: Export activity has no Sign in, and after a revoked or expired sign-in the saved token still exists, so the workspace shows the mailbox as ready with no Sign in button; the only way is Overview, Clear sign-in, reload, Sign in, which nothing names (8 steps walked) (F2, F3). (3) The card of a finished export offers neither Look again, Locate nor This folder is gone, although the text refused with when the owner renames or moves the folder tells them to click exactly those (F4). (4) A retry cannot be pointed at another destination: the engine takes one, the page never sends one, and "choose another destination" or "Click Browse" are the remedies of a full or missing stick (F5). The keyboard and screen-reader cell fails on the tree itself (F6).

## Findings

Ship blockers first (F1-F6), then the rest, most valuable first. All are in this branch's scope (the Extraction workspace, Export activity, the engine's user-visible outcomes). Keys cite the cells above.

**F1 · [Type III · in scope · blocker · cells OW-3, OW-5, OW-6, OW-10, OW-12] A refusal from a card button is shown and then wiped.** *(script)*
`act()` in `exports.js` builds `done=d=>{if(!d.ok)out.appendChild(el('div',d.error,...));loadHistory();}`. `loadHistory()` empties `#history` and rebuilds every card, including the `.cardres` the error was appended to. Finish as partial, Check and finish, Look again, This folder is gone, Locate… and the three "Export ... again" buttons all end in `done`, so R-b, R-c, R-l, R-e, R-h, R-check, R-r, R-r2, R-d2 appear for one round trip and vanish. Only Open folder and Check this export keep their text (they do not call `done`). The owner clicks and nothing happens.
*Change:* keep a result on its card across the rebuild (re-attach by run id, or skip rebuilding a card that holds a result), and mirror the refusal into the page's `role="alert"` banner. Add a test that clicks through a refused card action and asserts the text is still in the DOM after the next history answer.

**F2 · [Type III · in scope · blocker · cells OW-2, OW-5, OW-6] "Sign in again" names a control the owner cannot reach.**
R-c, R-b and R-z all say to sign in (R-c adds "then click 'Export the remaining messages' on Export activity"). Export activity has no Sign in (the page loads no `signin.js`; walked). The workspace shows Sign in only when no sign-in is saved; after a revoked or expired refresh token the token file is still there, so `source` answers `ready`, the page says "Mailbox: alice (your own mailbox)" and Load folders refuses R-c in a loop (walked). The Overview says "Signed in as alice [Clear sign-in]". The engine does not need the token cleared: the sign-in runs against a fresh in-memory store (`signin.go`). The only route is Overview, Clear sign-in, reload, Sign in, code, back (8 steps), named nowhere.
*Change:* when the saved sign-in is refused (R-c, or a refresh `invalid_grant`), have `source` answer `needs-sign-in` and show Sign in on the workspace and on Export activity (load `signin.js` there); the run's R-c/R-z note and the card get a Sign in button.

**F3 · [Type III · in scope · blocker · cells OW-2, OW-6] The retry's refusals give the wrong remedy.**
R-l on a retry says "Load folders again to start a new selection" (`RetryInput` reuses the selection wording), but the owner has no selection; the fix is to sign in as alice. R-b says "Click Sign in and enter the code shown" on a page with no Sign in. R-c at retry says only "Sign in again, then try again".
*Change:* retry-specific wording: "You're signed in as bob@..., but this export is of alice@...'s mailbox. Sign in as alice@..., then click Export the remaining messages." Fold with F2's button.

**F4 · [Type III · in scope · blocker · cell OW-10] A finished export's card has no Look again, Locate… or This folder is gone.**
For a settled run the reconciler never probes, so the card shows Open folder and Check this export only (walked, also after a restart). When the owner renames, moves or deletes the folder, both refuse R-r2 ("Click Look again on Export activity") or R-r ("click Locate… ... if it's lost, click This folder is gone"), and neither button is on the card. The engine does it correctly (Locate through the API gave "Complete (recovered)", gone gave "Export these 7 messages again"). Moving a finished export folder is the most ordinary thing an owner does.
*Change:* offer the three buttons on a settled card after Open folder or Check refuses R-r/R-r2 (or always, under a "Folder moved?" group), and fix R-r2's "(the drive may have been plugged in again ...)" for the plain "not found" case ("that folder isn't there any more").

**F5 · [Type III · in scope · blocker · cells OW-9, OW-8] A retry cannot be pointed at another destination.** *(script)*
R-g2 ("choose another destination") and R-e ("Click Browse to choose one") are the remedies of a full or missing stick, but `exports.js` posts `{runId, which}` only, and the retry preview opens on the workspace with the Destination card hidden until Load folders (which a retry never needs). The engine accepts `dest` for a retry; walked through the API (part 2's preview went to the other folder).
*Change:* show the destination (path box, Browse, Create folder) on the retry preview and send it; keep the part naming. Design §7.2 lists "choose another destination" for both the full and the pulled stick.

**F6 · [Type III · in scope · blocker · cell OW-19] The folder tree and the page's announcements are mouse-only.** *(script)*
Folder names (`span.name`) and expand arrows (`span.arrow`) are click handlers on spans: no tab stop, no role, no key. A keyboard user cannot open a folder to see its messages (so no Select current folder, no per-message ticks) and cannot reach a subfolder (the children are in a hidden list). Folder and message checkboxes have no accessible name (the name is a sibling span). The filter input has a placeholder and no label. `aria-live`, `role="progressbar"` and `role="tree"` appear nowhere: the selection count, the preview, the running progress and every card result are silent. `loadHistory()` rebuilds all cards every 5 s and after each action, which drops keyboard focus. The Cancel, card and Start buttons are real buttons, and the page has headings, landmarks and `role="status"`/`"alert"`, so the rest is reachable.
*Change:* a tree with `role="tree"`, focusable rows, arrow-key expand and Enter to open; `aria-label` on each checkbox from its row; a live region for the count, preview, progress and card results; no rebuild of a card that has focus. Re-walk with a screen reader and no mouse.

**F7 · [Type III · in scope · cells OW-8, OW-11, HE-4] The failed messages are not shown or explained anywhere an owner or helper looks.**
Design §7.2 promises "1 message not exported (subject, folder, reason)". The card says "4 messages not exported" and README says "4 were not exported"; neither lists them or says why. The manifest's `failures` array holds subject, folder, class and reason, and nothing points to it. With the destination turned read-only mid-run, 4 messages failed and neither the card, the README nor the page said why.
*Change:* a "Which ones?" line on the card (subjects, folders, reasons, from the run record) and a README section that names the manifest's `failures` list.

**F8 · [Type III · in scope · cells OW-5, OW-7, OW-8, OW-20] A stopped run's reason and remedy are lost, and raw system words get through.**
After R-g with the folder gone, the History card's label is "folder not found (at 09:36)" and its note is gone: the stop text with its remedy exists only on the live "Finished" card. The R-g text carries "(openat: no such file or directory)", R-w "(mkdirat: permission denied)", and R-g counts messages attempted ("stopped after 7 of 7 messages") while 0 were exported; an R-c card shows the refused message as "1 failed". R-w leads with Windows Security advice on a Mac and never says "write-protected or read-only".
*Change:* carry the sealed or recorded stop text onto the observation card; translate OS errors ("the folder isn't there", "the disk is full", "the folder is write-protected"); count exported, not attempted; do not count a sign-in refusal as a failed message.

**F9 · [Type II · in scope · cell OW-11] Offers are per run, so a completed set keeps offering to export it again.**
After part 3 completes the set, part 1 still offers Export the remaining messages (previews the 5 already exported, into `-part4`) and part 2 still offers Export failed messages again (the 2 already exported). Clicking either duplicates messages and moves the set fingerprint. Older cards keep "Finished with problems — 2 messages not exported". The design chose per-run record reads (XC2); deriving the offer from the chain's remaining set does not weaken the control.
*Change:* subtract what later parts exported before offering, show "Part of a complete set" on the earlier cards, and warn in the preview if the messages are already in a part.

**F10 · [Type III · in scope · cells OW-11, HE-3, HE-4] Nothing says a set is complete, and the set cannot be checked without the app.**
No README or card states the set's total. Part 1's marker and README never say other parts exist; a helper handed part 1 cannot know where the other 4 messages are. The set fingerprint orders parts by *seal* order (an interrupted part 1 finished after part 2 sealed changes it) and no folder records that order; Check this export checks one part.
*Change:* write "Set total: N selected, M exported, in K parts" into the README of the last part and the receipt, record seal order in the manifest chain, and add "Check the set" to the app. Part 1's marker, once a retry exists, can say "continued in <folder>" when the folder is next touched.

**F11 · [Type III · in scope · cell OW-14] An export paused for a capture says nothing.**
While the weekly backup or Archive now holds the archive lock the export backs off (uncounted by the 5-minute breaker). The page shows "0 of 7 done, 0 failed" and "0 B written" for as long as the capture runs; the log has only "Started". Cancel works.
*Change:* progress carries `waiting: "the weekly backup is running — your export continues when it finishes"` and the page shows it.
