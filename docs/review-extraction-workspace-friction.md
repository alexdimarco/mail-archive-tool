**Rationale.** What held up in the first walk still holds: the happy path is short and honest (11 steps from a first visit including the Microsoft code, 8 once signed in, and the preview says what, how big, where and who can read it), an interrupted run is recoverable with nothing lost, Check this export names the file that changed, and a helper with no app can verify the folder with the README's own commands. What failed was the road back, and it is rebuilt: a refusal from a card button now stays on its card and in the alert (F1); the sign-in that every "Sign in again" names is a button on Export activity, shown when the saved sign-in is refused, and the workspace no longer calls a refused sign-in "ready" (F2, F3); a finished export whose folder was renamed, moved or deleted gets the Look again, Locate and This folder is gone buttons its refusal tells the owner to click (F4); a retry can be sent to another destination (F5); the folder tree is made of buttons and named controls with live regions and a progressbar (F6). The re-walk then found five things the first fixes did not reach (F19 at the Preview, the "stopped after 7 of 7" count, stale labels on a whole set's earlier parts, the workspace's Sign in after a refusal, a dead tab's frozen card) and they are fixed too. What is not built, and why, is the Disposition: R-s's remedy and the README's first line are pinned by existing tests and the engine's own R-l text by another, and the set-level help for a helper (a note in a sealed earlier part, the seal order in the manifest, "Check the set") is new behaviour of the sealed format, so each waits for the owner. The fresh-eyes check then found what the re-walk could not: the sign-in script reloaded the pages that now always carry the Sign in button, about once a second, after any completed sign-in (F28), plus R-k, R-e and stop-line words that still pointed at the wrong button, a Windows path or no remedy (F29-F31); all four are fixed and the guards for two of the first fixes were widened.
